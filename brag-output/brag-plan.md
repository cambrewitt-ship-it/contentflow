# /brag plan — Content Manager

**What it is:** AI social media software for marketing managers & agencies: brand-voice AI writes the posts, clients approve them in a shared portal, and it schedules to every platform.
**For:** Agencies, freelancers and in-house marketers juggling many brands.
**What sets it apart:** Brand-voice AI, a calendar, client approvals and scheduling, all in one tool.
**Boldest claim (their own copy):** "Manage weeks of social media content in 10 minutes."
**Visual hook:** The hero headline slams in word by word. "weeks" and "10 minutes" are in blue italic, the same as the site.
**Share line:** Weeks of client content, written in their voice, approved and scheduled before your coffee's cold.

## Tone & identity
- Tone: `default`. Punchy, clean and confident, with soft transitions.
- Poppins 400–900 (the app's own fonts), primary #2563eb, ink #111827/#374151, the app's light gradient (#fff → #e0f2fe → #7dd3fc), and the CM gradient logo.
- UI is rebuilt from the real screens (Content Creation suite, client portal column view, Schedule button, social-icons.tsx). All copy is taken from the screenshots and the landing page.

## Sound
An original 120 BPM track in C major is synthesized from scratch (soft kick, hats, bass, Cmaj7–Am7–Fmaj7–G pads, pluck arp). The clicks are tuned soft plucks in the same key, the reveal and outro get a chime on the C chord, and the transitions get filtered-noise swells. Everything goes through one shared reverb and a gentle master compressor.
